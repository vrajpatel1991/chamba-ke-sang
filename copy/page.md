### The two bases

Chapter 1. The river base
H2O House sits by the Saal river below Chamba, five huts built over the water. The host collects guests from the Chamba bus stand.

Chapter 2. The walk in
The ridge is reached by road to Rota, then about 2 km through deodar. Bags are carried up. With small children, stay at the river.

Chapter 3. The ridge base
Mystic Village is a 200-year-old Gaddi village at about 2,200 m, five mud-and-slate houses and two cottages, run as a community homestay.

## record.html

### Hero

The Chamba Local Spend Record

One page by email within 7 days of checkout, showing what the stay paid people in Chamba.

### What it counts

It reports what was paid. A payment is counted when a bank or UPI reference shows it reached an account in the provider's own name.

Direct Local Payment Rate
Payments to verified Chamba providers, over the total the guest paid.

Local Value Retention
The same, plus payments to locally owned businesses in Chamba district.

Providers paid
How many distinct payees the journey paid.

### Hypothetical example

Hypothetical example. These numbers are made up to show the shape of the record, not an estimate of any journey.

### What is excluded

Cash with no reference, operator markups, and anything not yet paid. NotOnMap's fee, GST and gateway costs are shown on every record, and never counted as local.
