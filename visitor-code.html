The visitor code

Five rules, given before arrival and signed at check-in. They are the villages' rules, not ours.

Read the code

Five rules

  • Ask before every photograph

    Of a person, a house or a kitchen. A no needs no reason. Children are never photographed.

  • Follow each house's rules

    On alcohol, on dress, on where shoes come off. They differ from house to house.

  • Carry your waste out

    Everything that came up the hill with you goes back down with you.

  • No cash or sweets for children

    Pay the household, through us, for what you were given.

  • Enter shrines and kitchens only when you are invited

    Both are somebody's, and an invitation is not assumed.

Why it is written down

A village that hosts strangers carries the cost of every misunderstanding. Writing the rules down means a guest can follow them without being corrected, and a host does not have to ask twice.

Any provider can pause an experience by telling the coordinator, with no reason needed. A village meeting can close any experience. We take a paused item off sale within a day.
