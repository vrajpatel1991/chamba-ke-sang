What this site collects

The hold-dates form, and nothing else. There is no tracker, no advertising tag and no cookie on this site.

Read what we keep

The hold-dates form

  • What it asks

    Your organisation, a contact name, an email, a phone number if you give one, the kind of partner you are, the months, the group size, the journey and your message.

  • Who reads it

    NotOnMap, which is Unknown Destinations Pvt Ltd, in Chamba. Nobody else.

  • Where it goes

    A sheet NotOnMap owns. Nothing is passed to an advertiser or a data broker.

  • How long it is kept

    Two seasons, then it is deleted unless you have become a partner.

  • How to have it deleted

    Reply to any message from us and ask. We delete it within a week.

What this site does not do

No analytics script runs on these pages. A link we send in an outreach message may carry a campaign tag, which arrives with your form entry and tells us which message you opened. Nothing follows you after you leave.

The photographs on this site are loaded from this site. The one thing a page asks of another company is its two typefaces, from Google Fonts.
