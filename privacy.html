The ask-about-dates form

What it asks
Your name, an email, which version of the trip you want, how many of you there are, the months that suit you and anything else you want to tell us.
Who reads it
NotOnMap, which is Unknown Destinations Pvt Ltd, in Chamba. Nobody else.
Where it goes
A sheet NotOnMap owns. Nothing is passed to an advertiser or a data broker.
How long it is kept
Two seasons, then it is deleted unless you have travelled with us.
How to have it deleted
Reply to any message from us and ask. We delete it within a week.

What this site does not do

No analytics script runs on these pages. A link we send you may carry a campaign tag, which arrives with your form entry and tells us which message you opened. Nothing follows you after you leave.

Every photograph is loaded from this site. The one thing a page asks of another company is its two typefaces, from Google Fonts.
